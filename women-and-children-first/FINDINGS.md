# Findings

Data: the Titanic passenger list, Kaggle training set, 891 passengers (a
public historical record compiled from Encyclopedia Titanica). Children =
known age under 13. 177 ages are blank, so age figures count the 714 with an
age.

- Everyone: **38.4%** survived (342 of 891).
- Women **74.2%**, men **18.9%**.
- Age by decade: under 10 **61.3%**; every other decade 31.6-43.7%
  (70-79: 0% from only 6 people).
- Children under 13: **58.0%** (40 of 69).
- Class: 1st **63.0%**, 2nd **47.3%**, 3rd **24.2%**.
