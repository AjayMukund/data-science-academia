# A mile in no time by 2562? Extrapolation

A straight line through the 32 ratified men's mile world records (1913-1999) falls about 4 seconds a decade and reaches a mile in no time at all by 2562. Fitted only to the records up to Bannister (1954) it called 1999 to within a second; then the record stood for 27 years. From the [Wikipedia progression table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mile_run_world_record_progression) of World Athletics' ratified records.
