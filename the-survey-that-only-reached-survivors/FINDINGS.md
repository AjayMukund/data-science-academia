# Findings: the survey that only reached survivors

1,000 random samples of 100 passengers each (`random.Random(0)`).

| | the ship (891) | survivors (342) | random 100s, middle 95% | highest of 1,000 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| women | **35.2%** | **68.1%** | **26%-44%** | **51%** |
| first class | 24.2% | 39.8% | 17%-33% | 36% |
| third class | 55.1% | 34.8% | 46%-64% | 70% |

- The survivor survey is 3.4x larger than one random sample, and lands
  outside every random sample on women and on first class.
- Why: survival depended on sex and class, so who could be asked depended on
  exactly what was being measured. A bigger biased sample is not a better one.
