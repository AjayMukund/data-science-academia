# Findings: did money buy a lifeboat?

"Paid more" = fare above the median of the group being compared.

- Overall: paid more **51.8%** survived (230/444), paid less **25.1%** (112/447).
- Who paid more: **210** first-class passengers vs **6**; **45.9%** women vs **24.6%**.
  Women survived at 74.2%, men at 18.9%.
- Within class and sex, paying more went with higher survival in **three**
  groups (2nd women, 2nd men, 3rd men) and lower in **three** (1st women, 1st
  men, 3rd women).
- Third-class women: paid more **38.6%**, paid less **60.8%**. The higher
  fares covered families of 4.1 on average, against 1.3. By fare per person:
  **50.0%** (35/70) and **50.0%** (37/74).
- Not every group is that clean: per person, third-class men are still
  17.9% vs 10.6%. Data like this can point at a cause; it can't prove one.
